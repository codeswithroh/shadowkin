How it plays

An old trick, with a heartbeat

Shadow puppets only need a light and your hands. Shadowkin uses your real walls and tracked fingers, and lets the shadow live.

01

Sit, face a wall

A paper lantern lights up in front of you and throws warm light onto your real wall or desk. Everything is within arm's reach.

02

Raise a hand

Put the controllers down. Your tracked fingers cast a real, soft-edged shadow that grows as you move toward the light.

03

Fill the glow. Hold.

Shape your shadow to fill the glowing outline and keep still. It peels off the wall as a creature with its own voice.
